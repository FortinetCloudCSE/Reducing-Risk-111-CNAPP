## Welcome!

In this workshop you will go through several labs where you learn about some of the challenges securing the public cloud.  If you're part of a live session your instructor will let you know when to progress through the following labs. If you are going through this content asynchronously on your own, please click on "Introduction and Setup" in the left-hand navigation to continue.

Topics covered:
* Why does the Public Cloud exist and why is it so popular and how does it work?
* How has the Public Cloud impacted cybersecurity?
* What novel solutions exist for these novel problems?
* How can you use FortiCNAPP to enact those solutions in a live environment
