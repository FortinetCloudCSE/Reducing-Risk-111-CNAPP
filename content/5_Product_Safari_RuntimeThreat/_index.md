## Lab Overview

The goal of this lab is to get you hands on in the product interface with FortiCNAPP's Runtime Threat offerings (CWPP, UEBA, Anomaly Alerts and Composite Alerts). Your instructor will guide you through the product interface and inline challenges as you go collectively. Please feel encouraged to ask questions and interject as you progress (or ignore your instructor completely and click around -- just learn something!).

## Tools / Access

Accounts for FortiCNAPP will be handed out as part of the course. If you are doing this asynchronously from a live event, please reach out to either [Adam](mailto:alarson@fortinet.com) or [Gabe](mailto:gobrien@fortinet.com) to gain appropriate credentials:
